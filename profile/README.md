# COVERT

## People deserve better.

Clean and honest software, built to respect your machine and your time.

</div>

---

COVERT is a small, steadily growing software enterprise. We build focused, self-contained tools for Windows. No bloat, no hidden extras, no surprises: each tool shows you exactly what it will do before it does it.

We'd rather ship one thing that's genuinely good than a handful that are merely loud.

## Our tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[INVENTORIZER](https://github.com/COVERT-Enterprises/inventorizer)** | Snapshots your installed software, drivers and system state, shows what changed over time, and can rebuild a machine with a winget restore script. |
| **[FORGE](https://github.com/COVERT-Enterprises/forge)**<br> · *private beta* | Prepares clean Windows 11 installs: debloat the PC you're on, or bake the cleanup into a USB installer. Preview-first, with a restore point and undo. |

More on the way.

## What "better" means to us

- **Respect.** Your machine, your data, your time. Read-only where possible, reversible where not.
- **Honesty.** Plain language, clear previews, and changelogs that say what actually changed.
- **No bloat.** Self-contained, zero dependencies, nothing installed you didn't ask for.
- **Quality over hype.** We release when it's ready, not before.

## Downloads

Each tool ships through its own **Releases** page, with a ready-to-run download and a full changelog. Versions follow `vYY.MAJOR.PATCH`, so `v26.2.0` is the second feature release of 2026.
